## How they fit together

**CARE** is the user-facing consumer at the top of a four-module stack:

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="MAGE — generation" icon="seti:python">
		Turns a natural-language task into a CARL chain.
	</Card>
	<Card title="CARL — execution" icon="rocket">
		Runs the chain: DAG parallelism, RAG context, tools, memory, multi-agent.
	</Card>
	<Card title="GigaEvo Memory — persistence" icon="document">
		Stores chains, agents, skills, and run history.
	</Card>
	<Card title="GigaEvo Platform — evolution" icon="random">
		Runs a genetic search over chains and promotes the winners.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>

In short: **CARL is the engine, CARE is the cockpit.** You can use CARL on its own as a library, or drive the whole ecosystem through CARE.
